# BMW E9x K-CAN (body CAN)

Frames on the K-CAN of the E9x, with their definitions where they are known. Membership comes from a 2011 E92 M3 DCT (Europe) trace, plus K-CAN frames that the superwofy notes describe but that car did not send. Generated by `build_kcan_dbc.py`; the DBC is [BMW_E9x_K-CAN_full.dbc](./BMW_E9x_K-CAN_full.dbc).

The MK60E5 DSC is not connected to K-CAN. The junction box (JBE) is the gateway between PT-CAN, F-CAN and K-CAN; it copies many PT-CAN frames, including DSC frames, to K-CAN for the instrument cluster, iDrive and body modules, usually at a slower rate. See the [bus topology](./MK60E5_DSC–CAN-Interface.md#bus-topology) in the CAN interface description.

## Cross-check of DSC signals

The car was parked with the engine running; it rolled a little forwards and backwards and the steering wheel was turned. The K-CAN copies of the DSC frames therefore give the first check of signals that change while moving:

* 0x1A0 V_VEH up to 2.0 km/h; 0x1A0 V_VEH vs. mean of the 0x0CE wheel speeds while rolling: 112 samples, median difference 0.03 km/h, max 0.29 km/h
* wheel-speed sign vs 0x1A0 ST_VEH_DVCO: forward (>0.3 km/h) -> 0: 11, forward (>0.3 km/h) -> 1: 44, reverse (<-0.3 km/h) -> 0: 20, reverse (<-0.3 km/h) -> 2: 37, standstill -> 0: 5072
* steering: 0x0C4 (DSC) raw angle equals 0x0C8 (SZL) in 472/564 frame pairs within 20 ms (ratio 1.004 at large angles; differences only while turning); angle range -181.7..163.8 deg at 0.04395 deg/bit
